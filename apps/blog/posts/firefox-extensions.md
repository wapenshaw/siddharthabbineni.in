Now that Firefox 3 has been out for a while, most of the extensions that you used in Fx2 will work in Fx3. Here is a list of all the extensions that I use and recommend to others. They are many, but they are all worth it. Here is the list :

Enabled Extensions: \[33\]

-   [Adblock Filterset.G Updater](http://www.pierceive.com/) 0.3.1.3
-   [Adblock Plus](http://adblockplus.org/) 0.7.5.5
-   [Adblock Plus: Element Hiding Helper](http://adblockplus.org/) 1.0.5
-   [All-in-One Sidebar](http://firefox.exxile.net/aios/) 0.7.6
-   [Better Gmail 2](http://ginatrapani.org/workshop/firefox/bettergmail2/) 0.6.1
-   [ColorZilla](http://www.colorzilla.com/) 2.0
-   [Ctrl-Tab](http://en.design-noir.de/mozilla/ctrl-tab/) 0.19.1
-   [CuteMenus - Crystal SVG](http://www.cutemenuproject.com/) 1.9.3
-   [Find Toolbar Tweaks](http://homepage3.nifty.com/georgei/extension/ftt_en.html) 2.0.0b2
-   [Firebug](http://www.getfirebug.com/) 1.2.0
-   [FlashGot](http://flashgot.net) 1.1
-   [Gmail Manager](http://www.longfocus.com/firefox/gmanager/) 0.5.5
-   [Google Reader Watcher](http://ajnasz.hu/google-reader-watcher) 0.0.12.1
-   [Greasemonkey](http://www.greasespot.net/) 0.8.20080609.0
-   [IE Tab](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/1419) 1.5.20080823
-   [Java Console](http://www.google.com/search?q=Firefox%20Java%20Console) 6.0.07
-   [keyconfig](http://mozilla.dorando.at/) 20060828.1
-   [LiveClick](http://projects.protej.com/liveclick/) 0.3.0.0
-   [Locator](http://locator.jacos.net) 3.0.20080730
-   [MeasureIt](http://www.kevinfreitas.net/pro/extensions/) 0.3.8
-   [Menu Editor](http://menueditor.mozdev.org/) 1.2.6
-   [MinimizeToTray](http://minimizetotray.mozdev.org/) 0.0.1.2006102615+
-   [MR Tech Toolkit (formerly Local Install)](http://www.mrtech.com/extensions/) 6.0.1
-   [Paste and Go 2](http://pasteandgo2.mozdev.org/) 0.8
-   [PicLens](http://www.piclens.com/) 1.8.0.4280
-   [Screen grab!](http://andy.5263.org/screengrab/) 0.95
-   [Secure Login](https://blueimp.net/mozilla/) 0.9.2.8
-   [Smart Bookmarks Bar](http://remy.juteau.free.fr) 1.4.2
-   [StumbleUpon](http://www.stumbleupon.com/) 3.26
-   [Tab Mix Plus](http://tmp.garyr.net) 0.3.6.1.080416
-   [TimeTracker](http://www.usablehack.com) 1.2.4
-   [Toolbar Buttons](http://codefisher.org/toolbar_button/) 0.5.0.5
-   [Web Developer](http://chrispederick.com/work/web-developer/) 1.1.6

Out of those I would strongly recommend all Adblock extensions, Gmail Manager, keyconfig, Liveclick, Piclens (CoolIris), Secure Login, Tab mix plus. And if you are a web devloper, Firebg and Web Developer toolbar are a must. The others are just either cosmetic or tweaks, but they make a hell of a difference when browsing.
