Whoa! Long time since the last blog! Well that’s my weakness. Lots of stuff happening, treats, farewells age2x tourneys, websites, repairing websites, playing god with paper, treats, farewells and more…

The semester is coming to an end! That means…. Majors! Everyone starts getting high on mugging again and I can’t stand people complaining about how less they have mugged. I can’t seem to understand the psychology of people here; they mug all night and still are jealous even if others just touch a book. Oh well, I guess I have to ignore that and just go on with my ‘studies’ (ROFL!)

Under pressure from ‘Attila the Hun’ and for my own thirst for being an Admin for an international clan site, I started working on a site for our Age of Empires Clan, called ‘theHNClan’ here [http://www.thehnclan.tk](http://www.thehnclan.tk/) . As I am a huge phpbb fan, I used IntegraMOD for the site and it turned out extremely well as you can see on the site.

And finally I am an admin for an international site :D. I recently defeated ‘Attila the Hun’ in a match, and now he’s all like “I want REVENGE”, what he doesn’t seem to get it is that I just don’t care. He seems so violated by that defeat. (make sure you check out [www.thehnclan.tk](http://www.thehnclan.tk/) for his rants :)) )

Ah, coming to the ‘big’ events, there is nothing like a treat in a high class restaurant. The outgoing batch of 2001 were kind and generous enough to give us a treat in Waves, where were able to “order whatever we wanted”, which later proved very costly for them, hehe, the total bill came out to around 11k!!!! for 30-odd people. It seemed no one was satisfied with that and then after a week or two (4 days ago) the 3rd yearites planned a farewell for the to-be pass-outs, this time it was a restaurant near the India Gate, then after the dinner it was “kheench” time, everyone of the seniors had to tell their experiences in IITD, Saran started… and then the guard there kicked us out coz it was 11pm and no was allowed to stay there after 11pm. Good for us. >:)
