Yesterday, my friend and I were discussing about Age of empires : The Conquerors. He being new to the game wanted to learn. I advised him to watch some recorded games of players on IGZones.

There are hundreds of recorded games on [AoCZone](http://www.aoczone.com). As he wanted to download many games at once, he didnt want to click on the download button every single time. So I made him this small script so that it downloads the game automatically the moment you visit the page.

Download : [Auto RG Download Script](http://www.siddharthabbineni.com/wp-content/uploads/2008/08/aoczonenetdirectrecordedgamedownload.user.js)

**NOTE : You must have [Greasemonkey](http://www.greasespot.net/ "Greasemonkey") Installed!**
