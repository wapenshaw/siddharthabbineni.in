We are still alive!

With the LHC now running successfully, we have nothing to fear! At least till collisions take place.

I had no idea about this nor CERN’s Big Bang experiment till now and I thought Digg covered everything! The only news thats on Digg these days is about the US Elections. While I was in the process of getting pissed off at Bill-O and others, CERN switched on the LHC.

And obviously I didn’t hear any of the rumours that were circulating either. “The end of the world”, “The creation of a mini black hole which will end the world”. Scary eh? Ironically I learnt about all these rumour after finding out about the experiment.

I’m glad we are still alive and I hope we will be! CERNists please remember, there are no real super heroes to “Save the world”. On the other hand I’d really like to know more about our universe. Give us what we need to thwart the retarded “Believers”!
