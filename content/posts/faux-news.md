I went on the Internet and I found this : [Jon Stewart - Sarah Palin and how the Republicans see her](http://www.youtube.com/get_video?video_id=NYNfJ9SabFU&t=OEgsToPDskLOOQVgpVutMPQhkyF_rFHL "Click here to download the video"). I think it was Digg that led me there. Bloody Digg.

My first reaction was “ROFL”. Then I thought, “Really? is all that true?” and then it all started. I went on a night-long YouTube marathon going through every video in the Related videos section and then the videos in the Related videos section of the related videos.

“The Presidential Race”, frankly, I don’t know anything about it except that Hilary lost and that now its between Obama and McCain. I never really understood their system, so I never bothered. But anyway, the Internet as it seems, is playing a major role here. There are thousands of videos YouTube and thousands of articles blogs websites which are dedicated to represent or propagate its/their views to the masses.

As I went through the endless stream of videos, the one thing I noticed was that the media was biased heavily. Especially, Fox news. Well, Fox apparently is Pro-Right and Anti-Left. Their shows, the news they report and ways how they manipulate them to support the Republican views are staggering. The videos bring out clear evidences to support the allegations and are very convincing. I myself never trust anything that is on the internet unless it is from a legit source, however all the videos I watched were clippings from TV channels, so they are real. I must say I’m still only partly convinced, around 75%.

From what I observed in the videos, Fox blindly supports Republicans and any Democratic issue is dealt in an unfair fashion. There are hundreds of controversies surrounding Faux (I prefer to call it that from now on). Also they are more biased towards Christianity, something which I can’t usually digest. If you see this video [**Fox** News Trashes the **Netherlands**](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKuJvYh6h9I "Fox News Trashes the Netherlands") you will see how warped their perception is of the world that doesn’t go with their views. They are so bothered about how Christianity is losing ground and that is what pisses me off. All the people there are having fun, no doubt whatsoever, so why the fuck aren’t you happy? Answer, “Hey my religion isn’t doing really well”. I’m stunned by all this, that a news channel that is supposed to report news is so extremely biased. Just search for “fox news” is YouTube and view a couple of videos you will understand better. Its not just fox news MSNBC apparently roots for the Democrats, but for every video against MSNBC there are 500 videos against Faux. Wanna know more? Click » [Here](http://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=fox+news&search_type=&aq=-1&oq=fox+new "Faux at its best")

Bill O’Reilly, The O’Reilly factor, No spin zone are some phrases came up during the marathon. I will concentrate on Bill O’Reilly. He is a man, a talk show (The O’Reilly factor) host on Faux news to be specific. Oh God! Where should I start? OK, I have decided not to describe him in my own words. Why? Well, every sentence that I will use to describe him will inadvertently include loads of profanity. Something like F\*\*\*ing, B\*\*\*\*\*d, S\*B, id\*\*t,st\*\*\*d, \*\*\*\*\*,\*\*\*\*\*,\*\*\*\*\*\*\*,\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*,MFer etc etc. So I won’t. Peace dude. However I would like to reflect upon his show and his character in the show. There are some major points I’d like to bring out. First and the foremost, every argument with this him will involve him shouting at the top of his voice and then concluding he was right irrespective of opponents argument or conclusion. And in debates where he clearly loses, he ends them, turns to the viewers and then mocks the other guy at his will. What a load of crap. [Evidence](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H7Nt8MQaKko&feature=related "Oh god..")  
At every stage he seems to contradict himself. The Iraq issue with him is just a comedy strip everytime.

> Why did we go there? - WMD. Were there any WMD? - No it was misinformed by the CIA, it was a mistake. Why don’t we come back then? - No we need to give the people a chance to experience democracy. By sending troops? Yes its a war on terror.

The other thing is he defends almost all his claims by using the “American” sentiment. Go see the him interviewing Michael Moore [Part 1](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=trOdCU6aZOg "Michael Moore vs. Bill O'Reilly Pt. 1of 2") [Part 2](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fLHI0b43xa4 "Michael Moore vs. Bill O'Reilly Pt. 2of 2") you will know what I’m talking about. Its just plain bullshit, whatever that comes out of his mouth. Wanna know more? Click » [Here](http://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=o%27reilly&search_type=&aq=0&oq=O%27 "Billy")

Ann Coulter. What a bloody racist bitch. [Ann Coulter wants Jews “to be perfected”.](http://www.youtube.com/get_video?video_id=2wnPHFSdrME&t=OEgsToPDskLoGNxB41_y2Fnp5zz9wW2D "Click here to download the video") Oh my God! They must kill Ann Coulter! How the hell did she get away with that? Who supports her? Knowing about her existence really scares me. What kind of a world are we living in? When there are people who support her when she says stuff like that, the world is definitely not a pleasant place any more. Wanna know more? Click » [Here](http://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Ann+Coulter&search_type=&aq=f "Bitch in action")

I was gonna write about my new Swift but I had so say something after that marathon. Thanks Bill and Ann and Faux for all the entertainment.
